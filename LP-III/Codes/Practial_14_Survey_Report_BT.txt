## BC-4: Survey Report on Types of Blockchains and Their Real-Time Use Cases

Aim: To study different types of blockchain technology and understand their real-world applications.

### 1. Introduction

Blockchain is a distributed digital ledger that stores transactions in blocks. These blocks are connected using cryptographic techniques, making recorded information difficult to modify. Blockchain provides transparency, security, and decentralization.

### 2. Types of Blockchains

1\. Public Blockchain

- Open to everyone.
- Anyone can participate in transactions and network validation according to network rules.
- Provides transparency and decentralization.
- Examples: Bitcoin and Ethereum.

2\. Private Blockchain

- Controlled by a single organization.
- Only authorized users can access the network.
- Provides privacy and controlled access.
- Example: Hyperledger Fabric in enterprise applications.

3\. Consortium Blockchain

- Managed by a group of organizations.
- Access is restricted to selected participants.
- Suitable for collaboration between multiple organizations.
- Example: Banking and supply chain networks.

4\. Hybrid Blockchain

- Combines public and private blockchain features.
- Sensitive information remains private while selected information can be publicly verified.
- Provides flexibility and controlled transparency.
- Example: Enterprise record management systems.

### 3. Real-Time Use Cases of Blockchain

| Application      | Use Case                                        |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Banking          | Secure cryptocurrency transactions and payments |
| Healthcare       | Secure sharing of medical records               |
| Supply Chain     | Tracking products from manufacturer to customer |
| Education        | Verification of certificates and degrees        |
| Voting           | Recording votes using blockchain                |
| Real Estate      | Maintaining property ownership records          |
| Digital Identity | Secure identity verification                    |
| Smart Contracts  | Automatic execution of agreements               |

### 4. Advantages of Blockchain

1. Provides transparency in transactions.
2. Improves data security.
3. Reduces the risk of unauthorized modification.
4. Supports decentralized applications.
5. Provides traceability of transactions.
6. Automates processes using smart contracts.

### 5. Limitations of Blockchain

1. High transaction costs on some networks.
2. Scalability and performance challenges.
3. Requires technical knowledge.
4. Privacy and regulatory concerns.
5. Energy consumption can be high in some consensus mechanisms.

### 6. Conclusion

Thus, different types of blockchain technology were studied along with their real-world applications. Blockchain provides secure, transparent, and reliable solutions in various industries such as banking, healthcare, education, and supply chain management.
